# Midterm Project - "Starlight"
Morgan Lincicum

This scene is based on a video game concept I came up with a few years ago. The game features three odd creatures who gained sentience after the stars fell out of the sky and crashed into their planet. Upon picking up a magical star shard, they gain the power to do things such as move objects with their mind. 
In this scene, the first character, Brobinson, is exploring a flower field riddled with fallen stars that have pierced the earth and finds a magical star shard on a podium. The position of the fallen stars and flowers is random each time the page is loaded. The star shard lets out a pulsing glow. Use WASD to move Brobinson around the field. He is a curious creature who will look wherever you're pointing. 

You can also use the mouse to move the camera, as described by the text on screen. 

## Features
### 8+ Objects
The field contains 60 flowers, 7 stars, the podium (3 objects), the star shard, and Brobinson (many objects)
### 4+ Geometry Types
BoxGeometry, CylinderGeometry, CapsuleGeometry, TorusKnotGeometry, PlaneGeometry, and SphereGeometry were used
### 3 Different Materials
Standard, Phong, and Physical were used
### Ambient Light, Spot Light, Point Light, Shadows Enabled
A spot light is above the podium and point lights emanate from each flower and the star shard. Shadows are enabled. 
### Translation Demonstrated, Rotation Demonstrated, Scaling Demonstrated
All objects are first created as a basic shape at 0,0,0 then rotated, translated, and scaled.
### 3 Animations
Brobinson's walking animation, his eye movement, and the pulsing glow
### 3 Keyboard Controls
WASD and mouse
